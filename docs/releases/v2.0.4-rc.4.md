### What changed in rc.4

**Evidence nobody can wave away**

- **Run it 24/7 on a Raspberry Pi, a home server or Docker.** A laptop that sleeps
  leaves "not monitored" gaps; an always-on device doesn't. New downloads:
  `internet-monitor-headless-linux-arm64` (Pi 4/5), `-armv7` (Pi 2/3) and `-amd64`.
  It prints a private link and you open the dashboard from any phone or PC at home.
  A `Dockerfile` and `docker-compose.yml` are included. Steps: `docs/headless.md`.
- **Sealed logs.** Every finished day is sealed: the SHA-256 of each log file, chained to
  the day before. Changing any old file, or any older seal, breaks the chain. The monthly
  report has a new "Log integrity" card.
- **Independent timestamp (optional, off by default).** Settings → App → "Timestamp the
  sealed logs" sends each day's seal hash (32 bytes, never your data) to freetsa.org and
  keeps its RFC 3161 reply. Anyone can then check with `openssl ts -verify` that your logs
  existed in that exact form on that date. The evidence ZIP includes the seals and the
  commands.
- **Wi-Fi or cable.** Every minute and every outage now records whether you were on a
  cable or Wi-Fi, with the Wi-Fi signal. The report says "Measured over a cable to the
  router" (or the mix), each outage says if it was over Wi-Fi and how strong the signal
  was, and the complaint text says the measurements were wired. This answers "it's your
  Wi-Fi" before they say it.
- **Better power-cut detection.**
  - An outage that starts while a laptop is running on battery is marked "most likely a power cut, not the provider".
  - A gap during which the computer booted is labeled "turned off or restarted", no longer just "asleep or app closed".

### How to test

- [ ] Raspberry Pi or Linux: download the headless file, run it with
      `--listen 0.0.0.0`, open the printed link from your phone. Without the
      `?session=…` part the dashboard must refuse.
- [ ] Docker (if you use it): `docker compose up -d --build`, then `docker logs internet-monitor`
      shows the link.
- [ ] Leave the app running past midnight. Next day the report shows "Log integrity: 1 day
      sealed". Edit yesterday's `logs/metrics_*.jsonl` by hand: the card says a file changed.
- [ ] Turn on "Timestamp the sealed logs", wait for the next day (or restart the app the
      next day), download the evidence ZIP, and run the `openssl ts -verify` command from its
      README: it prints "Verification: OK".
- [ ] On a laptop: unplug the charger, then unplug the router. The outage row says the computer
      was on battery.
- [ ] On Wi-Fi: cause an outage. The row says "Measured over Wi-Fi, signal N%". On a cable,
      the report header says "Measured over: a cable to the router".

### Known limits

- The Docker image has not been built in CI yet. It builds the same way as the headless
  binaries, but needs a first build on your side.
- macOS doesn't expose the Wi-Fi signal without admin rights, so on a Mac only Wi-Fi vs
  cable is recorded.
- Desktops without a battery can't tell a power cut from the router failing, so they don't
  get the battery note. The boot-time check still works.
- Timestamps use freetsa.org by default. You can point `tsa_url` in `config.json` at
  another RFC 3161 authority.
