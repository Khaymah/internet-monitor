### What changed in rc.7

**What the router saw during each outage**

- The app reads your router over UPnP once a minute, on your home network only: the
  line to the provider (up or down), the line's sync speed, the internet session, how long
  the router has been running, and its traffic counters. A new "Your router" card on the
  overview shows them.
- Each outage in the monthly report and in the complaint evidence now adds what the router
  saw:
  - the line to the provider was down (a fault on the provider's line),
  - the line was up but the provider dropped the internet session,
  - the router restarted,
  - or the router stayed connected, so the fault was further inside the provider's network.
- The evidence ZIP includes `logs/router_*`. Settings → App → "Read the router's status"
  turns it off (on by default).

**Data plan**

- Complaints → line details takes the plan's monthly data limit and the day it renews.
- The router card shows how much you've used since the renewal day, the days left, and
  whether it will last at this pace.
- An alert goes out at 80% and at 100% of the limit, once each per month, through the
  channels set for "Weekly summary and reminders".
- "What your data says" adds three tips: the line itself dropped (strongest point for a
  complaint), the router keeps restarting, and the data won't last the month.

**Three more languages**

- French, Turkish and Urdu (right to left), for every screen, alert and report.

**Releases**

- Microsoft Store publishing is paused. Releases ship on GitHub only; the Store package
  is no longer built or attached. `docs/microsoft-store.md` says how to resume. Store
  screenshots and listing text are in `docs/store/` and `docs/store-listing.md`.

### How to test

- [ ] Turn on UPnP in the router (often under Network or Advanced) and wait two minutes:
      the overview shows "Your router" with the model, line state and sync speed. Turn
      UPnP off: after about three minutes the card says no router answered.
- [ ] Unplug the phone/DSL cable from the router for a minute, then plug it back. Next day's
      report row for that outage says the line to the provider was down.
- [ ] Restart the router from its power button: the outage row says the router restarted.
- [ ] Complaints → line details: set a data limit lower than what you've used this month
      (for example 1 GB). Within 10 minutes you get the "data plan used up" alert, and not
      again after that.
- [ ] Switch the language to Français, Türkçe and اردو: every page reads in that language,
      and Urdu is right to left.

### Known limits

- Not every router offers UPnP status. Many provider routers turn UPnP off by default, and
  some (including some ZTE and Huawei models) don't report the line sync speed or the
  traffic counters. Without counters there's no data-plan tracking.
- Many routers keep 32-bit counters that wrap every 4 GB. Reading once a minute catches
  the wrap at home speeds; while the app is off, traffic over 4 GB can be missed.
- The usage is what passed through the router, so it can differ slightly from the
  provider's own meter.
- The French, Turkish and Urdu texts are machine translations checked for keys and
  placeholders, not yet by native speakers.
