### What changed in rc.2

**Complaints now use what the app measured, not just what you type.**

- **"Your line in the last 7 days"** at the top of the Complaints page: outages,
  downtime, longest outage, uptime, how much of the week was monitored, and speed
  against your plan. If the line dropped, it offers to log a complaint with those
  numbers already in the notes.
- **Every complaint shows the evidence**:
  - A verdict line: "Still dropping: N outages since you filed" or "No outages since you filed".
  - "Evidence and complaint text" opens the 7 days before you filed next to
    everything since, so you can see whether anything changed.
- **Ready-to-send complaint text**, written from those numbers in your language
  (provider, line, period, outages, longest outage, speed vs plan, coverage, your
  earlier complaint number). You can edit it, copy it, and download the evidence
  ZIP for exactly that period.
- **Not Egypt-only any more.** Pick your country in the line details (Egypt, Saudi
  Arabia, United Kingdom, United States, or other). The escalation steps, the
  regulator and the provider's response window follow it, and you can set your
  own number of days.

### How to test

- [ ] Complaints → line details → pick a country → Save. The "How to escalate"
      card and the response window change with it.
- [ ] Set "Days the provider has to respond" to 1 and save: an older open
      complaint shows the "escalate now" warning.
- [ ] On a complaint, open "Evidence and complaint text": both columns load and
      the numbers match the History page for those days.
- [ ] "Copy text" puts the text in the clipboard. Paste it in WhatsApp or an email
      and check it reads well in Arabic and English.
- [ ] "Download evidence (ZIP)" on a complaint saves a ZIP whose logs cover only that period.
- [ ] With a few outages in the last 7 days, "Log a complaint" opens the form with
      the numbers in the notes.

### Known limits

- The Saudi 5-day response window and the CST steps come from secondary sources.
  Check them against the CST site before 2.0.4 is final.
- The UK and US steps are general guidance and don't replace the regulator's site.
