### What's new in 2.0.4

The biggest update since 2.0: Internet Monitor now turns what it measures into a complaint
your provider can't brush off, reads your router to say whose fault each outage was, and
has a website and short videos that explain everything in plain words. Everything below
was tested over fourteen preview releases (`v2.0.4-rc.1` … `rc.14`); each one's notes are in
`docs/releases/`.

**Complain with proof**

- A Complaints page built on your own measurements: outages, downtime and speed against your
  plan for the 7 days before you complained and since, a ready-to-send complaint text, a
  proof file (ZIP) and the right next step for Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UK or the US.
- "What your data says" turns your data into plain tips, and a weekly reminder suggests
  complaining when the line keeps dropping and no complaint is open.
- The monthly report estimates what the downtime cost you, shows the worst hours of the day,
  lists the time nothing was being checked, and compares your speed tests with your plan.
- Each finished day of records is closed with a digital seal (optionally with a trusted
  timestamp), so anyone can check nothing was changed.

**Know whose fault it was**

- The app reads your router (UPnP, and on Huawei HG630 V2 and similar routers its own page
  if you add the password): line speed, noise margin, why the last disconnect happened, and
  for every outage whether the provider's line, the internet session or the router failed.
- Wi-Fi or cable, power cuts, and where the route stopped are recorded with each outage.
- Data plan tracking from the router's counters, with alerts at 80% and 100%.
- The speed test grades how much slower the line gets while busy, A+ to F.

**Easier to use**

- Plainer words everywhere, in English, Arabic and now French, Turkish and Urdu.
- A short narrated video for every page ("How this page works").
- A Mac installer: open the `.dmg`, drag the app to Applications. It works on every Mac.
- A Raspberry Pi / home server / Docker version that watches the line around the clock, opened
  from your phone with a QR code.
- Alerts through ntfy and any webhook, an outage timeline, and a weekly summary every Monday.
- A website with the download, a picture guide and the videos:
  https://khaymah.github.io/internet-monitor/

**Updating**

The app offers this update by itself. On Windows and Linux it replaces itself and restarts.
If you use the Mac app from the new `.dmg`, the update opens the download page instead.

### Known limits

- Windows and macOS show a one-time warning because the app isn't signed with a paid
  certificate; the website's install steps show what to press.
- Reading the router's own page is built for the Huawei HG630 V2 family; other routers show
  what they report over UPnP.
- The French, Turkish and Urdu texts and video subtitles still need a native speaker's review.
