### What changed in rc.5

**Complaints and compensation**

- **What the downtime is worth.** Add your monthly price and currency in Complaints →
  line details. The monthly report and the complaint text then show the pro-rata value
  of the time you had no internet ("at 310 EGP a month, 9 hours offline is worth
  3.88 EGP"). Outages that look like power cuts are left out. In the UK, outages longer
  than two days are counted as Ofcom automatic-compensation days.
- **When it breaks.** The report has a new chart of downtime by hour of day and says how
  much of it fell between 7pm and midnight. The speed card compares the typical speed in
  the evening with the rest of the day.
- **What your data says.** A new card on the overview and the Complaints page turns
  the last 30 days into plain advice, with a button where it helps:
  - low monitoring coverage
  - outages that stopped right after your router (the provider's side)
  - DNS-only failures, with a suggested fix
  - outages clustered at the same hours
  - bad lag under load
  - weak Wi-Fi, or measuring over Wi-Fi
  - power cuts
  - speed far below your plan, or much slower evenings
  - "you had N outages and no complaint yet"
- **Complaint reminder.** If the line keeps dropping (3 or more outages in a week) and no
  complaint is open, you get one "Time to complain?" alert, at most once a week. It goes
  wherever the weekly summary goes.
- **Send it in one tap.** Next to "Copy text", the complaint text can now go straight to
  WhatsApp or an email draft.

**Microsoft Store package**

- CI can now build `internet-monitor-store.msix`, the window app packaged for the
  Microsoft Store. It starts building once the Partner Center identity is filled into
  `packaging/msix/identity.json`; see `docs/microsoft-store.md`.
- When the app runs from the Store package:
  - It keeps its data in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\InternetMonitor`.
  - It leaves updates to the Store.
  - It starts at sign-in through Windows' own startup setting.
  - It sends notifications under the package's identity.
  - Normal installs don't change.

**Privacy policy**

- New `PRIVACY.md` (English and Arabic) at the root of the repository, for the Store
  listing's privacy policy URL. The in-app Privacy page now uses the same text and lists
  what was added since 2.0.3: `case.json`, ntfy, the weekly summary, optional log
  timestamps (freetsa.org), the outage traceroute, and the Store version's data folder.

### How to test

- [ ] Complaints → line details → set a monthly price and currency → Save. The report's
      "What the downtime is worth" card and the complaint text show an amount.
- [ ] The report's "Downtime by hour of day" bars match the outage times in the table.
- [ ] The overview shows "What your data says". With a few outages and no open complaint,
      it suggests filing one and the button opens the Complaints page.
- [ ] Set your plan speed far above your real speed and run 3 speed tests: the
      "Your speed is N% of what you pay for" card appears.
- [ ] On a complaint, "WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with the text, and "Email" opens a new
      email with the subject and text.
- [ ] With 3+ outages in the last 7 days and no open complaint, a "Time to complain?"
      alert arrives within an hour, and not again for a week.
- [ ] Privacy (footer) lists case.json, ntfy, freetsa.org and traceroute, dated 5 Nov 2026,
      in English and Arabic, and matches `PRIVACY.md`.

### Known limits

- The compensation figure is an estimate to support a claim, not a legal entitlement.
  The Ofcom daily rate (£10.34) is the published figure at the time of writing; check
  ofcom.org.uk for the current one.
- Peak hours are fixed at 7pm–midnight in this rc.
