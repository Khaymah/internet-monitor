### What changed in rc.14

**Mac: a real installer**

Mac downloads are now `.dmg` files, one that works on every Mac (Apple chip and Intel), so
there is no "which Mac do I have" question and no Terminal step:

- `internet-monitor-macos.dmg`: Internet Monitor, which runs in the background with a small
  icon at the top of the screen (recommended).
- `internet-monitor-gui-macos.dmg`: Internet Monitor Window, which opens like a regular
  program.

Open the file, drag the app onto Applications, open it. The app isn't signed with a paid
Apple certificate, so the first time macOS may say it can't verify it: System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (once). The website's Mac steps say exactly that.

When the app runs from Applications it keeps its records in
`~/Library/Application Support/Internet Monitor` instead of next to the program, and an
update opens the download page rather than replacing the app's file from inside. The raw
Mac files are still on the release (and on the website under Advanced) for anyone who
prefers them.

**Wording**

- Arabic says "شاشة" (screen) wherever it used to say "شباك" or "نافذة" (window), in the app
  and on the website.
- Arabic "اللاج وقت الضغط" (lag under load) is now "البطء وقت الضغط", in the app, the speed
  test video's description and its Arabic subtitles.
- The website's videos page opens with one short line instead of repeating itself.

### How to test

- [ ] On a Mac (Apple chip or Intel), download `internet-monitor-macos.dmg`, drag the app to
      Applications and open it; after "Open Anyway" once, the icon appears at the top of the
      screen and "Open dashboard" works. Records go to
      `~/Library/Application Support/Internet Monitor`.
- [ ] Same for `internet-monitor-gui-macos.dmg`: the window opens and shows the dashboard.
- [ ] https://khaymah.github.io/internet-monitor/ar/download/ on a Mac shows one button for
      the `.dmg`, no chip question, and the four plain steps.
- [ ] The app in Arabic: the speed test says "البطء وقت الضغط"; the tray menu says "افتح الشاشة".

### Known limits

- The Mac "can't verify" prompt stays until the app is signed and notarised with an Apple
  Developer ID (paid).
- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
