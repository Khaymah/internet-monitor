### What changed in rc.1

- **Complaints page** (sidebar → Complaints): your provider, plan speed, line and
  account number, and a log of every complaint you filed. Open complaints count
  down the provider's 72-hour response window, then suggest escalating to the
  regulator (Egypt's NTRA steps).
- **Monthly report**:
  - Monitoring coverage %, and a "Not monitored" list for times the computer was off or asleep.
  - A gap that ends straight into an outage is marked as a likely power cut.
  - Speed tests compared with your plan speed.
  - Your line details and the month's complaints.
- **Evidence pack**: "Download evidence pack" on the report saves a ZIP with the
  report, raw logs and a SHA-256 checksum for every file.
- **Settings → Speed test** shows how much data each test and the schedule use.

### How to test

- [ ] Complaints → fill in your line details → Save. Reopen the page: they are still there.
- [ ] Log a complaint, mark it escalated, then resolved, then delete it.
- [ ] Report → your provider and plan show under the title.
- [ ] Report → "Download evidence pack" saves a ZIP **inside the app window** (WebView2).
- [ ] Put the computer to sleep for 10+ minutes, wake it, and open the report: the
      sleep shows under "Not monitored", not as an outage.
