### What changed in rc.9

**Tutorial video**

A narrated tour of the Settings page (about 7 minutes, English voice) explains every
setting, section by section: what it does and why you'd change it.

- Open it with "Watch the settings tour" at the top of Settings, or "Settings tour" in the
  sidebar next to FAQ and What's new.
- Subtitles in English, Arabic, French, Turkish and Urdu come with the app; the one for the
  app's language is on by default.
- The video itself isn't inside the app. It's a file on this release and streams from
  GitHub only when you press play. If it can't load (offline, or not published yet), the
  page says so and offers to try again. The privacy policy lists it.

**A website for the app**

`https://khaymah.github.io/internet-monitor/`, in the app's own look and in all five
languages, written for people who aren't technical:

- Home: what the app does and why it helps, with screenshots.
- Download: the right file for your computer first, beginner install steps (including the
  Windows SmartScreen and macOS "unidentified developer" warnings), and a separate box for
  a newer preview version.
- Guide ("your first complaint in 5 steps" and running it on a Raspberry Pi), the tutorial
  video, FAQ, privacy and what's new. FAQ and privacy are read from the app's own text.
- It rebuilds itself on every release, so download links and notes are always current.

### How to test

- [ ] Settings → "Watch the settings tour": the video plays with the subtitles in the app's
      language, and the subtitle menu offers all five languages. With the internet off,
      pressing play shows a message with "Try again".
- [ ] After this release is published and Pages is turned on (repository Settings → Pages →
      Source: GitHub Actions), open the website on a computer and a phone: the Download
      page offers this release's files, and the Video page plays the tour.

### Known limits

- The narration is a synthetic English voice; the French, Turkish and Urdu subtitles and
  website text are machine translations that still need a native speaker's check.
- The website deploys only after the one-time Pages setting above.
